## 2026-09-03 - Confirmation Dialogues and ARIA Tags for Delete Actions
**Learning:** Destructive actions like deleting an entry from the ledger were being executed immediately upon clicking the trash icon button. This icon button was also missing proper ARIA labels and focus indicators, making it poorly accessible for screen readers and keyboard users. Furthermore, since the deletion relies on Firebase, standard local tests may fail without auth initialization unless the dialog logic is placed before the auth check.
**Action:** Added `window.confirm()` prior to the Firebase authentication check to ensure the dialog appears even during unauthenticated local testing. Improved accessibility by adding `aria-label="Delete entry"`, `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-red-500` styles, and `aria-hidden="true"` on the purely decorative internal SVG icon.
